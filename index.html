About Me

My journey began before October 17, 2025, when I was 11 years old. Even without a computer, I was already making project plans, sketching ideas, and imagining how to bring them to life.

On the night of October 17, 2025, everything changed. My parents thought: "What if we give this computer to our son?" My mom suggested it, my dad said "Maybe," and that decision opened the door to my future. The very first thing I installed was the official Roblox Studio — my gateway into creating and programming.

From that moment, I started building and experimenting. By late 2025, I had begun learning HTML, moving from game creation into web development. In less than a year, I grew from a beginner to a creator, building projects and laying the foundation for my enterprise.

In 2026, I launched my official website and founded RStar Technologies, a solo-driven enterprise focused on innovation, software development, and AI solutions. Every line of code, every design, and every project is built by me — proof that one person can spark big change.

My vision is simple: to keep learning, keep building, and keep pushing the boundaries of what’s possible. This is just the beginning.
